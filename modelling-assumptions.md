# Modelling Assumptions

## Purpose and scope

The workbook is an independent and illustrative public-information LBO model prepared as of 24 March 2025. It is not Clearlake Capital's actual underwriting or financing structure. The principal case excludes information first published after the announcement date.

All monetary amounts are in US dollars and millions unless stated otherwise. The model uses a five-year holding period and measures gross sponsor returns before fund-level fees, carried interest and investor-specific expenses.

## Entry valuation and sources and uses

- Equity purchase price: $4.1 billion, as announced.
- Transaction value: $7.7 billion including outstanding debt, treated as the headline enterprise value.
- Offer price: $9.15 per share.
- Debt refinanced or assumed: $3,550.4 million of year-end 2024 principal.
- Cash acquired: $205.9 million at 31 December 2024. Most cash was held outside the United States, so unrestricted availability requires diligence.
- Transaction fees: assumed at 2.0% of disclosed transaction value.
- Financing fees: assumed at 2.0% of illustrative debt raised.
- Entry adjusted EBITDA: FY2024 management adjusted EBITDA of $926.6 million.
- Base entry debt: 5.5x adjusted EBITDA, sensitized from 5.0x to 6.0x.
- Sponsor equity: residual amount required for sources to equal uses.

The rounded announced equity and transaction values do not exactly reconcile to year-end debt and cash. The model preserves the announced figures and separately shows fees rather than forcing an artificial bridge.

## Operating assumptions

The 2025 base case starts from the midpoint of management guidance: $2,470 million revenue and $970 million adjusted EBITDA. The model then applies annual revenue growth and EBITDA-margin assumptions through FY2029.

| Assumption | Bear | Base | Bull |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Revenue growth after FY2025 | 1.0-2.0% | 3.5% | 4.5-5.0% |
| FY2029 adjusted EBITDA margin | 39.5% | 40.8% | 42.2% |
| Total capex and software investment / revenue | 8.5% declining to 7.6% | 8.0% declining to 7.2% | 7.8% declining to 6.8% |
| Cash taxes / adjusted EBITDA | 10.0% | 10.0% | 10.0% |
| Change in net working capital / revenue change | 1.0% | 1.0% | 1.0% |
| Recurring cash-cost reserve / adjusted EBITDA | 2.5% | 2.0% | 1.5% |

The cash-cost reserve recognizes that management excluded recurring categories such as equity compensation, restructuring and transformation costs from adjusted EBITDA. It is not a complete sponsor-defined EBITDA reconciliation because public disclosure does not identify every recurring element.

## Debt assumptions

- Term Loan A: 0.5x EBITDA at 7.0%, with 5.0% annual mandatory amortization of original principal.
- Term Loan B: 4.5x EBITDA at 7.5%, with 1.0% annual mandatory amortization of original principal.
- Senior notes: 0.5x EBITDA at 8.5%, with no scheduled amortization in the five-year period.
- Cash sweep: 100% of cash available after cash interest, cash taxes, total capex, working capital, recurring cash costs and mandatory amortization.
- Interest: calculated on the average balance before optional repayment. This avoids circularity and is conservative because cash-sweep repayments do not reduce current-year interest.
- Minimum cash: $100 million policy assumption. The sources-and-uses cash treatment remains illustrative and requires confirmation.

## Exit and returns

- Bear exit multiple: 7.0x.
- Base exit multiple: 8.3x, approximately equal to the $7.7 billion headline value divided by FY2024 adjusted EBITDA. The base case therefore assumes no material multiple expansion.
- Bull exit multiple: 8.5x.
- Exit enterprise value equals exit EBITDA multiplied by exit multiple.
- Exit equity value equals exit enterprise value less exit net debt.
- Gross MOIC equals sponsor proceeds divided by initial sponsor equity.
- Gross IRR equals MOIC raised to one divided by the five-year holding period, less one.

## Base-case interpretation

The model produces approximately 2.50x gross MOIC and 20.1% gross IRR. This reaches the bottom of the target range but leaves little room for underperformance. The bear case produces approximately 1.40x MOIC and 7.0% IRR. The investment therefore warrants only conditional approval pending retention, earnings-quality, maintenance-investment and debt-term diligence.

## Key limitations

- Final acquisition debt terms were not publicly disclosed at announcement.
- Gross and net retention, contract duration, price-volume mix and product-level profitability were not comprehensively disclosed.
- Maintenance versus growth software investment was not disclosed.
- The model is an LBO cash-flow model, not a fully linked GAAP three-statement forecast.
- No add-on acquisitions, management rollover, dividend recapitalizations or interim sponsor distributions are included.
- Retrospective merger-proxy forecasts and closing details do not influence the principal case.
